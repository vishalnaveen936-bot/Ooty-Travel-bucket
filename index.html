⭐ Featured Experience

Discover Ooty Botanical Garden

The Government Botanical Garden is one of Ooty's well-known attractions. The garden covers around 55 acres and features lawns, rare trees, flowering plants, ferns, glass houses and an old fossilized tree.

It is also a great location for photography, especially when flowers are in bloom.

  • 🌳 Large landscaped garden
  • 🌺 Colourful flowers and plants
  • 🌿 Fern and orchid collections
  • 🪨 Ancient fossilized tree
  • 📸 Great photography opportunities
